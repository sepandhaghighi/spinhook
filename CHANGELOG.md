## [0.1] - 2026-xx-xx
### Added
- Initial public release of SpinHook
- Core orbit-to-orbit grappling gameplay
- Dynamic orbit generation with progressive difficulty
- Infinite vertical progression with smooth camera tracking
- Particle effects and camera shake for gameplay feedback
- Main menu, HUD, and Game Over screens
- Local high score saving
- Responsive desktop and mobile support
- Touch and mouse controls
- SEO metadata and PWA support
